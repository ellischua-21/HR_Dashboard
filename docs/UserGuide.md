## 1. Sign in and get started

1. Open the dashboard link provided by your company.
2. Enter your account email and password, then select **Sign in**. Your account
   email is the Personal Email recorded when your account was requested.
3. Use the menu on the left to open a page. Select a group name to show its pages.
   On a small screen, select **Menu** to open the menu.
4. Check your **Inbox** for announcements and requests that need your attention.

An ADMIN must approve a new account before it can sign in. If you need an account
or cannot remember your password, contact HR or the system administrator.

## 2. Find your menus

STAFF, SUPERVISOR, and HEAD accounts open on **Dashboard** after signing in.
You can return to it using the first item in the menu.

- **Employee Profile** shows your main personal details. Select **View Full Profile**
  to expand the details recorded during employee registration. These details are
  read-only; ask HR if they need correcting.
- **Job Details** shows your job title, department, direct manager, work location,
  and start date.
- **Daily Attendance** shows your Time In, AM Out/In, Lunch Out/In, PM Out/In,
  and Time Out. Use the arrows to change day or choose a date from the calendar.
  Select **Refresh** to retrieve the latest records. A dash means no time is available.
- Select **Time Logs** at the top of Daily Attendance to see each scan, its device name,
  and device serial number. Click outside that box or press Escape to close it.

Your dashboard displays only your own profile and attendance. HR and ADMIN keep
their existing dashboard and attendance list.

| Account | Main features |
| ------- | ------------- |
| STAFF   | Fill out forms, check personal submissions, track incidents involving you, view case history and the Code of Conduct, read the Inbox, and change your password. |
| SUPERVISOR | STAFF features, plus review assigned STAFF form requests and file Incident Reports. |
| HEAD    | Personal dashboard and forms, assigned SUPERVISOR form reviews, Incident Report filing and assigned department incident reviews. Closed incidents are in Review. |
| HR      | Dashboard, employee records, attendance, reports, announcements, personal forms, form reviews, and incident filing and review. |
| ADMIN   | HR Performance Dashboard, employee records, attendance, reports, announcements, View Forms, account management, and assigned incident access. ADMINs acting as department HEADs can file/review IRs; designated Management approvers approve recommendations. ADMIN has no personal form submission pages. |

**Head HR is an HR account with an extra assignment.** It keeps the normal HR
features and can also review forms submitted by other HR accounts.

## 3. Use common controls

### Dates, times, and search

- Enter dates as **MM/DD/YYYY**. You can also use the calendar button.
- Enter clock times with **AM or PM**. Displayed dates and times follow Philippine time.
- Use filters to narrow a list. Check for an old filter if you cannot find a record.
- A field filled from your employee record may be locked. Ask HR to correct the employee record if those details are wrong.

### Signatures and confirmations

Draw your signature in the signature box using a mouse. Use **Clear signature** to start again, then select **Sign** or the form's submit button. Only sign while using your own account.

### Notification marks

- The **Inbox number** shows unseen items. Opening Inbox clears its count.
- Numbers beside form reviews or incident pages indicate pending requests or active cases. Reading an Inbox message does not resolve those requests.
- A small **yellow dot** on a closed Forms or Incident Management group means a
  page inside has a notification badge.
- Use the **↑** button to return to the top of a long page.

## 4. Submit and review forms

### Fill out a form

1. Open **Forms → Fill Out** and choose a form.
2. Check your automatically filled employee details.
3. Complete the remaining fields. Dates intended to record today's date are filled automatically.
4. Tick the confirmation and any form-specific acknowledgment boxes.
5. Draw your signature and select **Submit for review**.
6. Open **Forms → My Submissions** to check the saved request and serial number.

| Form                               | What to enter                                                                                                            |
| ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Leave Application                  | Leave type, dates, number of days, reason, and pay option.                                                               |
| Authority to Deduct                | The deduction details and amount, plus the authorization acknowledgment.                                                 |
| Time-In/Out Correction Request     | Dates and the time entries that need correction.                                                                         |
| Official Business / Travel Request | Business or travel details and the requested schedule.                                                                   |
| Overtime Request                   | The overtime schedule and reason.                                                                                        |
| Time In/Out Record                 | Daily time and break records.                                                                              

Paid Sick Leave and Vacation Leave allowances require **Regular** employment
status and an employee rank above **Rank and File**. Probationary employees and
Rank and File employees have no allowance. Eligibility uses the employee rank
recorded by HR, regardless of the account's login role.

Eligible employees receive five days of each allowance per calendar year, with
no carryover. The Leave Application shows the remaining balances and selects
Without Pay when the selected allowance is unavailable. Eligibility and balance
are checked again at final approval.

### Understand where a form goes

- STAFF forms go to the assigned Supervisor, then to HR for final review.
- SUPERVISOR forms go to the assigned Head, then to HR.
- HEAD forms go directly to HR.
- HR forms go to the designated Head HR. The Head HR's own forms go to another
  eligible HR reviewer.

### Check or edit your submissions

Open **Forms → My Submissions**. Filter by status or form type, or search using the
serial number or details.

- **Edit** appears only while editing is allowed. Generally, this is while the
  form is still pending with its assigned reviewer. Editing requires a new signature.

### Review a form request

1. Open **Forms → Review Requests**, or **View Forms** for ADMIN.
2. Use status, form type, search, and submission-date filters to find the request.
3. Select **View form** and read the request.
   HR must select **Take Task** before processing it. ADMIN can view requests and assign or reassign them, but cannot process them. If another
   reviewer has taken it, their name is shown and you can still view the form.
4. Select **Sign** when a signature is required, or **Approve** when it is not. Supervisor or Head approval normally forwards the request to HR.
5. To reject a request, select **Reject** and enter the reason.

When reviewing a Leave Application, HR can complete the **For HR Use Only**
details in the approval or rejection window. Select **Refresh** to reload the queue.

## 5. Manage employee records

### Find, view, and update an employee

1. Open **Employees → Employee Master List**.
2. Search for the employee or use the available company, department, and status
   filters.
3. Open the employee's record to view their profile.
4. Select **Edit**, make the changes, then select **Review and save**.
5. Check the preview and select **Save employee**.

Authorized accounts can also export the employee list using its export button.

**Archive** removes a record from normal active lists without permanently deleting
it. Read the confirmation before archiving an employee.

### Register an employee and request account access

1. Open **Employees → Employee Registration**.
2. Complete the employee details, including their employee number and Personal
   Email.
3. Select the correct **Reports To** suggestion: a Supervisor for STAFF or a Head for SUPERVISOR.
4. In **Account access request**, choose STAFF, SUPERVISOR, HEAD, or HR. Enter and confirm the
   initial password.
5. Select **Review and save**, check the preview, and save.
6. Ask an ADMIN to approve the request in **Manage Accounts → Account Requests**.

A STAFF account needs an assigned Supervisor, and a SUPERVISOR needs an assigned Head. If the reviewer has a pending account request, approve it first.

### Import employees from a spreadsheet

1. Open **Employee Master List** and select **Import**.
2. Use the supplied template if needed, or select your employee spreadsheet.
3. Match each spreadsheet column to the correct employee field.
4. Run validation and review the valid-row count and errors.
5. Correct the spreadsheet where necessary, then import the valid records.
6. Check the **number of employees imported** in the result.

Rows with errors are skipped during import. Importing employee records does not
replace the account-request and approval process.

## 6. View attendance

**Open:** Employees → Attendance. **Available to:** HR and ADMIN.

The list loads automatically for today. Records from all connected scanner
accounts appear in the same list, grouped by department.

1. Select a date, or use **Previous day** and **Next day**.
2. Enter an employee name or choose a **Department** or **Status**.
3. Select **Apply filters**.
4. Select **Refresh** to request the latest records.
5. Use **Previous page** and **Next page** for additional records.

Attendance is for viewing. To request a correction, use the appropriate time form
or contact HR. Scanner-account connections are managed by the system administrator.

## 7. Use Incident Management

### Pages by role

| Role | Pages |
| --- | --- |
| STAFF | Track, History, Code of Conduct |
| SUPERVISOR | File, Track, History, Code of Conduct |
| HEAD | File, Track, Review, Code of Conduct |
| HR | Track, Review, Code of Conduct; the designated Head HR also sees File |
| ADMIN | File, Track, Review, Code of Conduct; filing and HEAD review require a department assignment |

All accounts use the same circle tracker: **IR Filed > NTE Issued > Closed**.
Other actions keep the current stage. A case can contain several Incident Reports,
with a separate IR number and outcome for each reported employee. Use **Related
Incident Reports** in Track to switch between the reports you may access. The
case reaches Closed only after every report in that case has been closed.

### ADMIN: set up reviewers

1. Open **Manage Accounts > Accounts**.
2. Under **Incident Reviewer Assignments**, choose a HEAD or ADMIN for each
   department. One reviewer may cover several departments. The **Heads** department
   has two ADMIN reviewer slots.
3. Select any active ADMIN as the **Management Approver**.
4. Select the existing **Save Changes** button. Leaving the page without saving
   discards your edits.

Department assignments route reports filed by supervisors in those departments.
The initial mapping is Paso Warehouse—HEAD 1; Dalisay/E-commerce—HEAD 2;
Marketing and Procurement—ADMIN 1; Accounting and HR—ADMIN 2 (including employee
records labeled Human Resources); Printing—HEAD 3;
Sales—HEAD 4; Stores—HEAD 5. ADMIN 1 and ADMIN 2 are both assigned to **Heads**.
The startup applies a mapping only when it finds a matching active reviewer account;
it does not create accounts. Assign any departments left Unassigned after the
corresponding HEAD account is available.

Reports against HEAD accounts enter a shared queue for the two ADMIN reviewers
assigned to **Heads**. The same applies when a HEAD files against STAFF in their own
department. The filer, reported employee, and witnesses cannot take that review.
An eligible ADMIN selects **Take HEAD Review Task**; once one takes it, the other
ADMIN can still view the case but cannot take it. An assigned ADMIN may review
incidents and use the HR Performance Dashboard. An ADMIN without a HEAD assignment
cannot file or take HEAD review tasks, but may approve a case if selected as its
Management Approver.

Only the designated **Head HR** can file an IR as HR. That report goes to the
reviewer assigned to the HR department for the initial IR/NTE decision. After the
reported employee submits an explanation, an uninvolved HR account can take the
evaluation task.

### File an Incident Report

1. Open **Incident Management > File**.
2. Search for the reported employee and select the correct suggestion. Section I
   fills their name, position, immediate supervisor, and department automatically.
3. Complete Section II: incident title, What, When, Where, Who, Why, and How.
   Describe facts and circumstances without assuming motive.
4. Use **Add Witness** for witnesses. Witnesses are listed within the IR;
   they are not automatically treated as reported employees.
5. Attach supporting files where needed.
6. For another reported employee in the same case, select **File Another**.
   Shared incident details are copied for you to review and edit. Each employee
   receives a separate IR number and can receive a different outcome.
7. Select **File Reports** and confirm.

The involved employees and reviewing HEAD are notified. The former employee,
Supervisor/Head, and HR signatures on the initial IR are no longer required.

### HEAD: review an IR

Open **Incident Management > Review** and select **View** to open Track.
HEAD filters are **All**, **For Review**, **NTE Issued**, and **Closed**.
ADMIN filters are **All**, **IR Filed**, **NTE Issued**, and **Closed**.
HEAD and ADMIN see their assigned review cases, plus eligible shared-queue items.

For each IR, the assigned HEAD can:

- **Request More Evidence** from the filing supervisor, with a written request.
  The supervisor receives a notification and a response form with attachments.
  HEAD must wait for the response before another request, NTE issuance, or closure.
- **Prepare Notice To Explain**, completing Sections III, IV, and VI. The notice
  retains Sections I and II from the IR and includes Section V's explanation
  directive. Save a draft or draw the issuing HEAD signature and **Issue NTE**.
- **Close For Documentation**, recording the closure reason. This does not add a
  confirmed violation. Other reports in the same case remain independent.

### Employee: acknowledge and explain

1. Open the case from **Inbox** or **Incident Management > Track**.
2. Read the issued NTE and permitted evidence. **View Document** provides a
   formatted copy and the browser's **Print / Save As PDF** option.
3. Sign the receipt acknowledgment in Section VI. It confirms receipt, not
   admission of the allegation.
4. The response deadline is **five calendar days after receipt**, including
   weekends and holidays. Track shows the countdown. An outstanding notice has
   a reminder at most once per day.
5. Complete Section VII, attach supporting documents, and draw your explanation
   signature. You may **Save Draft** before submitting.
6. Select **Submit Explanation** and confirm. The submitted statement and
   signature are preserved and cannot be overwritten.

An employee can **Request An Extension** while an explanation is outstanding.
HR can approve the requested days, choose a different number of additional
calendar days, or reject the request. The employee can also request an
administrative hearing when submitting the explanation.

An overdue response is marked overdue and HR is notified. Overdue status does
not establish a violation or automatically impose a sanction.

### HR: evaluate, obtain approval, and close

HR sees cases from NTE issuance onward. Review filters are **All**, **NTE Issued**,
**For Review**, and **Closed**. A submitted explanation moves the item into HR's
For Review filter; the circle stage remains NTE Issued.

1. Open the case and select **Take Task** after the employee has submitted the
   explanation. Another HR may view it, but only its owner may process it.
2. Read the IR, NTE, employee explanation, and supporting records.
3. If necessary, **Request Information From Employees**. Select the involved
   employees and write a separate request for each. HR must wait for all responses.
   Each recipient can submit one response per request and must then wait for
   further instructions.
4. For an external hearing, choose **Schedule Hearing** and enter its details.
   Involved people are notified. Mark it completed only after the hearing has
   actually finished; processing remains paused until then.
5. Open **Review NTE And Recommend Action** for Section VIII. Record findings,
   review indicators, whether a violation was established, and optional remarks.
   For an established violation, select the severity. The system counts previous
   finalized violations of that severity and fills the corrective action from
   the configured matrix. A finding of no violation has no disciplinary action.
6. Choose an uninvolved Management approver and **Send For Management Approval**.
   The designated ADMIN can approve or return the recommendation with remarks.
7. After approval, HR selects **Issue Approved Final Decision**. The reported
   employee receives the decision and a popup notification. Finalized established
   violations now appear in the employee's violation history.
8. Select **Close Case**, enter the closure reason, and confirm.
   Involved people are notified. The shared case tracker reaches Closed when
   every IR in that case is closed.

If an approved recommendation needs revision, use **Revise Evaluation** before
issuing it. New Management approval is required afterward.

HR time starts only when the HR task is taken. Earlier HEAD review and employee
explanation time are excluded from HR backlog and timing. The timer counts only
Monday-Friday, 8:00 AM-6:30 PM, Philippine time, and pauses for requested employee
responses, scheduled hearings, and Management approval.

### Documents and confidentiality

- Reported employees may see Sections I-VII and their issued final decision.
- Other involved employees and the filing supervisor may see Sections I-IV.
- Assigned HEADs may see Sections I-VII and the permitted final decision.
- Authorized HR and assigned ADMIN reviewers may see Sections I-VIII.
- Information requests and their attachments are available to the selected
  recipient and authorized reviewers. Internal HR activity is kept separate from
  the public timeline.

Existing cases from the former workflow are labeled **Read-Only Legacy Record**.
Their original documents, signatures, statuses, and history remain available
within case permissions. They cannot be processed through the replacement
workflow or counted as current HR backlog.

### Code of Conduct and violation history

Open **Incident Management > Code of Conduct**. The left panel contains the
corrective-action matrix; the right panel contains finalized violations.
Pending allegations and unsubstantiated findings are not confirmed violations.
STAFF, SUPERVISOR, and HEAD see their own history. HR and ADMIN can search an
employee, select a suggestion, and choose **View Records**.

HR can edit matrix rows, use **Add Matrix Row**, and select **Save Changes**.
The matrix used for a recommendation is preserved with that case, so later
matrix changes do not rewrite previously approved findings.

## 8. Manage accounts

**Available to:** ADMIN.

### Approve or reject a new account

1. Open **Manage Accounts → Account Requests**.
2. Check the name, Personal Email, assigned role, and assigned reviewer.
3. Select **Approve** or **Reject** and confirm the action.
4. Use **Refresh** to update the request list.

Approval enables the requested account to sign in. Approve a pending reviewer before the accounts assigned to them.

### Change reviewer assignments

1. Open **Manage Accounts → Accounts**. The page heading is **Manage Accounts**.
2. Find the STAFF or SUPERVISOR account and change its assignment.
3. Select **Save Changes** button.

### Designate the Head HR

1. In the HR section, switch **Head HR** on for the selected HR account.
2. Only one HR account can be selected at a time.
3. Select **Save Changes**.

When the Head HR changes, pending HR forms assigned to the previous Head HR are
transferred to the replacement. If pending forms still need a reviewer, the system
prevents removing the assignment without an eligible replacement.

## 9. Change your password

1. Enter your current password.
2. Enter a different new password with **12–128 characters**.
3. Enter the same new password again and select **Update Password**.

## 10. View HR performance

**Available to:** ADMIN, on **Dashboard**.

Only HR accounts are included in the performance figures. ADMIN can view performance and assign or reassign permitted tasks, but cannot take HR tasks, process form reviews, or edit the COC matrix. Department HEAD duties and Management approvals performed by ADMIN are separate from HR performance.

1. Choose a date range, reviewer, and request type, then select **Apply Filters**.
2. Use the overview cards and charts to compare completed form reviews, reviewed
   incident reports, workload, and review times. Approvals and rejections both count
   as completed form reviews. **Incidents Reviewed** counts distinct incident
   reports each HR worked on during the selected dates. Multiple review actions,
   decisions, or closure actions on the same report count only once per HR.
   The top **Incidents Reviewed** count includes each report once across the
   selected HR accounts.
3. Use the **Reviewer** filter to focus on one HR account. Select **All Reviewers**
   to return to the full comparison.
   In **Current Workload** and **Reviewer Performance**, **Tasks Assigned** counts
   all unfinished tasks owned by that HR. **Tasks Waiting for Employee Response**
   counts the portion awaiting signatures, an explanation, or requested information.
4. In **Review Tasks**, use **View** to open a form or incident. **History** shows
   who took or handled the task, changes of reviewer, and recorded pauses.
5. Select **Assign** or **Reassign**, choose an authorized reviewer, enter a
   reason, and select **Save Changes**. Previous reviewers keep their recorded
   time in the history. Only the current owner can process the task.
6. **Incident Task Assignments** lists which HR took or was assigned each incident,
   the most recent assignment date within the selected dates, and whether they
   remain the current owner, transferred the task, or completed it.

**Total Backlog** counts all unfinished HR tasks, assigned or unassigned, including
tasks waiting for employee responses. **Unassigned Backlog** counts the portion
without an HR owner. Subtract Unassigned Backlog from Total Backlog to see how many
unfinished tasks have an owner. Completed tasks are excluded from both counts.
Both backlog cards cover all HR accounts and follow **Request Type**, regardless
of the Reviewer, date range, or Task List Status filters.

**Tasks Waiting for Employee
Response** means tasks awaiting signatures, an explanation, or requested
information. This count can overlap with Unassigned Backlog when an incident is waiting for employee
signatures before an HR takes it.

### Understand the timer

- The timer starts at **00:00:00** when a task is taken. It counts only
  **Monday–Friday, 8:00 AM–6:30 PM, Philippine time**. There is no separate lunch
  deduction or automatic public-holiday exclusion.
- HR sees their own task timer in each form review row and in the incident Track view.
- Time pauses while an incident awaits requested employee information, a hearing,
  or Management approval. It resumes when HR can act, and stops when the task is completed.
- The timer continues correctly when the page is closed or the server restarts.
- **Total HR Time** includes HR reviewers' recorded time; ADMIN activity is excluded. **Current Owner Time**
  includes only the current reviewer's share. Time is a measure of workflow
  turnaround, not a record of hours actively spent working on the item.
- Completion figures use the selected dates; current workload shows unresolved
  tasks now. Older reviews can appear in completion counts, but missing ownership
  and timing records are not guessed.

Claim and ownership updates normally appear on other open HR dashboards within
10 seconds. Inbox entries remain visible, while tasks owned by another reviewer
are excluded from their task-related notification counts.

## 11. Get help

| What happened? | What to do |
| -------------- | ---------- |
| I cannot sign in.                                   | Check the email and password. Ask whether your account has been approved and is active. Contact HR or the system administrator for password help. |
| A menu or button is missing.                        | Check that you are using the correct account. Features depend on your role, case involvement, and the request's current stage. |
| A name was typed but is not accepted.               | Type part of the name again and select the correct suggestion. |
| My employee details are wrong and cannot be edited. | Ask HR to correct your employee record. |
| I cannot find a form or case.                       | Clear old filters, check the serial/IR number, and refresh. Your account must be authorized to see it. |
| HR cannot continue an investigation.                | Check whether any requested employee responses are still pending. |
| Attendance is empty.                                | Check the date, name, department, and status filters, then select Refresh. If it is still empty, ask the system administrator to check the scanner connection. |
| Attendance shows an error.                          | Note the message and account number, if shown, and contact the system administrator. A failed connected account can prevent the combined list from loading. |
| A document preview does not open.                   | Allow popups for the dashboard in your browser and try again. |
| The dashboard cannot be reached.                    | Check your connection and ask the system administrator to check that the dashboard services are running. |
| A save or submission failed.                        | Correct the stated issue and try again. If the result is uncertain, check the saved list or case before submitting a duplicate. |

When requesting help, provide the page name, serial or IR number if applicable,
the action you selected, and the error message. Never include your password.
